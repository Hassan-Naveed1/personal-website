01 / MY STORY

Why software engineering?

My interest in software engineering comes from the combination of logic, creativity, and problem-solving. I enjoy understanding how systems work, figuring out how to improve them, and turning an idea into something people can actually use.

After graduating with First Class Honours in Software Engineering, I want to take the next step into a professional development role where I can apply what I've learned, contribute to real projects, and continue developing as an engineer.

I believe that becoming a good developer involves more than knowing a programming language. It means understanding the problem, writing maintainable code, testing solutions, learning from mistakes, and being willing to improve.

That's why I continue working on personal projects outside my formal education. They give me the opportunity to experiment with technologies, strengthen my practical skills, and explore ideas that interest me.

I'm looking for a junior or graduate software engineering opportunity where I can learn from experienced developers, collaborate with a team, take on meaningful challenges, and gradually grow into a stronger, more capable engineer.
